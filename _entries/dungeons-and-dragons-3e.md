    [HTML version of the 3.5 SRD](http://www.darkshire.net/jhkim/rpg/srd/srd_revised_html/index.html)

    [3.0 monster data in XML and SQL](http://www.darkshire.net/jhkim/rpg/srd/monsters/index.html)

    [3.0 spell data in XML and SQL](http://www.darkshire.net/jhkim/rpg/srd/spells/index.html)
