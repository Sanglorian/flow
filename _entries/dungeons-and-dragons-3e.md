    The third edition of D&D and the only one to be libre, D&D 3E had two versions: 3.0 and 3.5.

    [Revised (D&D 3.5) SRD](https://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=d20/article/srd35)

    [Original (D&D 3.0) SRD](http://www.opengamingfoundation.org/srd.html)

    [The Hypertext d20 SRD](http://www.d20srd.org/) contains all official libre rules for the 3.5 version.

    There are HTML versions of the 3.0 SRD at [Zombler.org](http://www.zombler.org/files/srd30a/) and [Dragon.ee](http://www.dragon.ee/30srd/)

    [HTML version of the 3.5 SRD](http://www.darkshire.net/jhkim/rpg/srd/srd_revised_html/index.html)
    [HTML SRD](http://www.enworld.org/forum/showwiki.php?title=d20:Revised-d20-System-Reference-Document)

    [3.0 monster data in XML and SQL](http://www.darkshire.net/jhkim/rpg/srd/monsters/index.html)

    [3.0 spell data in XML and SQL](http://www.darkshire.net/jhkim/rpg/srd/spells/index.html)

    This the game the OGL was written for.
